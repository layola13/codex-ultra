## Part 1: Privacy Policy

### 1. Fully Local & No Remote Control
* **Credentials & Configuration**: API Keys, terminal logs, conversation history, and settings are stored locally and are never uploaded to central servers.
* **No Remote Control**: This software contains **NO remote control mechanisms, backdoors, or remote administration capabilities**. All network endpoints bind strictly to local loopback/private networks.
* **No Permission Reallocation**: Designed solely for single-user local operation without multi-tenant management.

### 2. Third-Party API Connections
* External connections are strictly limited to user-configured third-party AI providers (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, etc.) or storage integrations (WebDAV, S3, Notion). No central traffic proxying exists.

### 3. Diagnostic & Operational Telemetry
* Optional anonymized diagnostic logs track crash stack traces locally to improve system stability.
